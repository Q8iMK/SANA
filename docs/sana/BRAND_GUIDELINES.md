# SANA Brand Asset Guidelines

## Color Palette

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Black | `#0B0B0F` | App backgrounds, dark squircle, banners |
| Dark Secondary | `#17171C` | Cards, panels, subtle gradients |
| Primary Gold | `#D4AF37` | SANA symbol, primary accents, active borders |
| Gold Highlight | `#E8C967` | Gradients, hover states |
| Gold Dim | `#9E8022` | Secondary rules, subtle borders |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Primary typography on dark surfaces, light squircle background |

## Logo & Icon Usage

- Use `branding/assets/sana-icon-dark.svg` (or `sana-icon-dark-1024.png`) on dark and neutral surfaces.
- Use `branding/assets/sana-icon-light.svg` on light surfaces where high-contrast dark mark is required.
- Maintain clear space equal to at least 15% of the icon width around the emblem.
- Do not distort, recolor outside the SANA palette, or combine with legacy Hermes/Nous logos.
